Questions, answered
What does it actually cost?

Nothing to join. You're only charged $97 in a calendar month where we deliver you 5 or more new customers. In any month with fewer than 5, you pay nothing. Cancel whenever you like.

Who are these customers?

Locals using WanderLoot for a surprise outing. They set their preferences and we reveal your venue as their mystery destination — so they're brand-new customers who weren't already planning to visit you, not discount-hunters who'd have come anyway.

Do I need any special equipment?

No. We send you a small printed code for your counter. Guests scan it on their own phone and show your staff a green confirmation screen. No tablet, app or login required by your team.

Can I change or pause my offer?

Anytime. You're in full control of the offer and the redeem-by time, and there's no lock-in contract — just let us know.

How is this different from EatClub or Groupon?

They charge every month regardless, or take a big cut of the sale. We only earn when we've actually delivered you customers — and we send people who didn't choose you, rather than discounting someone who was already coming.
